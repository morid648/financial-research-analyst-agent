## Deliberate omissions

- **No specific market prices are cited anywhere in the deck.** Numbers pulled from this session's live testing (e.g. a specific AAPL price) came from a sandboxed data feed tied to the environment's simulated date and are not verifiable real-world facts. Only *structural* correctness claims (a ratio was 100x off; a formula was hardcoded) are used, since those are true regardless of what the market is doing on any given day.
- **No fabricated URLs.** Presenter name (Anshul) and GitHub (https://github.com/morid648) supplied by the user; the live-demo link was removed at the user's request — none were invented.

## Build

Generated by `build_deck.py` (python-pptx + native PowerPoint animation XML). Rebuild with:

    .venv/Scripts/python.exe docs/presentations/build_deck.py

All animations auto-play on slide entry (no clicks). Derived figure: "11 of 17 Critical or High (65%)" on slide 4 = (7 + 4) / 17 from S1. The title-slide sparkline is decorative and carries no data. The slide 5 leverage scale (0–1.5) is a plain axis with no risk thresholds, so none were invented.

Verified by opening in PowerPoint via COM: 10 slides, every effect registered in each slide's main sequence (including the 3 motion paths), and every slide exported to PNG and reviewed visually.
