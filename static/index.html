The signal and scores are rule-based heuristics on reported P/E, beta, leverage and recent price direction. They summarise the data; they are not a recommendation. For news sentiment and historical risk, open the dashboard.

What's computed

Every figure traces back to a filing or a price.

Where the underlying data isn't reported, the metric shows N/A rather than an estimate.

Discounted cash flow

Five-year unlevered free cash flow from reported revenue and margins, with D&A, capex and working capital taken from the trailing cash flow statement. WACC via CAPM, plus a WACC × growth sensitivity grid.

Sixteen ratios

Valuation, returns on capital, solvency (including Altman Z) and cash conversion cycle, calculated from the income statement and balance sheet.

News sentiment

Recent headlines scored individually with FinBERT, then aggregated. Each article's score is shown alongside the source.

Historical risk

Annualised volatility, 95% historical value-at-risk and Sharpe ratio from a year of daily returns.

How it works

Prices, statements and news come from the configured market data provider (Yahoo Finance by default). Ratios and the DCF are computed from those statements on each request; nothing is cached from a static table.
The agent layer supports local inference through Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM, with local ChromaDB storage and sentence-transformer embeddings. Market data still requires network access to your chosen provider.
Yahoo Finance, Financial Modeling Prep, Alpha Vantage and OpenBB. Set DATA_PROVIDER and optionally DATA_FALLBACK_PROVIDER to switch.
A Dockerfile and docker-compose.yml are included. docker-compose up -d starts the API alongside Redis and ChromaDB.
